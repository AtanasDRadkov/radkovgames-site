Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Conveyor Switch Sort does not create accounts and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Gameplay progress, settings, and purchase entitlement status are stored locally on the device.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple’s handling of transaction information is governed by Apple’s own policies.

The app contains no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, location access, contacts access, camera access, microphone access, or photo-library access. Apple may provide aggregated App Store analytics and diagnostics to Radkov Games under Apple’s terms.

For privacy questions, email support@radkovgames.com.
